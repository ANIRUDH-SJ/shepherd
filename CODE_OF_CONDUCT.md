# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, specific, and
constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique the idea or implementation, explain
the evidence, and leave room for people to revise their work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method shown on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in context,
kept as confidential as practical, and may result in warnings, content removal,
temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, release
channels, and other spaces where a person is representing Shepherd.
